Plateful shows you the calories, protein, carbs and fat for menu items at restaurant chains, so you can pick what fits before you order.

FIND IT BEFORE YOU ORDER
- Search a chain, then a dish, and see the full nutrition breakdown
- Compare two items side by side
- Filter a menu by calorie ceiling or protein floor
- Save your usual orders for one-tap lookup

BUILT FOR EATING OUT
Calorie counters expect you to log after the fact. Plateful is for the moment you are standing at the counter: fast lookup, no logging, no account.

WHERE THE NUMBERS COME FROM
Nutrition data comes from MenuStat, a public dataset of chain restaurant menus
compiled by the New York City Department of Health from figures the chains
themselves published under federal menu labeling rules. Chains change portions
and recipes over time, so treat values as a guide, not a guarantee. Plateful is
not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any restaurant listed in the
app.

WORKS OFFLINE
Your saved orders and recent lookups are cached on your device, so the app answers even with no signal.

CLEAR PRICING
Search and lookup are free. One purchase unlocks side-by-side compare, menu filters and unlimited saved orders. No subscription and no weekly plan.

Plateful provides nutrition information for general reference only. It is not medical or dietary advice.
